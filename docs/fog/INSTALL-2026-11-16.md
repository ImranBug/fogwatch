## 5. Known limits, in plain words (tell the player)

- **Built on your 19 Turbo games.** No untouched test game is left, and normal mode has never been tried. Expect a refit after a few games; the numbers are logged for that.
- **A place is shown for about 10 seconds** (up to 25 where your team can see the ground). After that an enemy is a small icon with a timer above the minimap and no place. Enemies that respawned get a timer only.
- **The amber arrow is a maybe.** When it is up, that hero turns up near you within 10 seconds 2 to 3 times in 10. About one arrow in five is gone in under a second (one in three in a game with a lot of fighting), nearly always because the hero walked back into view; the red arrow takes over.
- **Sometimes there is an amber icon and ring but no arrow.** If a red, SMOKE or JOIN arrow already points that way, the amber one stays away for that cue. That is 3 to 4 in 10 cue seconds.
- **"Nothing for 2 seconds" is really 1 to 3 seconds.** Dota reports whole seconds.
- **Deaths you did not see can be wrong.** About 2 kills in 100 are not registered, and 4 times in 19 games a living enemy was shown dead for a few seconds. An enemy that buys back stays "dead" until it is seen.
- **Fewer PUSH calls** (5 instead of 12 in three games; most of the lost ones counted enemies that were not dead) and slightly more "3 missing" notes.
- **Two enemies that vanish on one spot** are drawn side by side, so one icon sits up to two thirds of an icon off.
- **The second-screen dashboard is unchanged**: it still shows "MISSING n" and the old ghosts, so the two screens can disagree.
- **Not measured:** the frame-rate cost over a live game, and the placement on the MacBook's own screen (checked only on a stretched 2560 recording). Watch the first game for both.
- **Takes effect after you restart the coach between games.** Until then the new overlay file draws exactly what today's does.
